# Checklist of the Birds of Kenya v2026.1

This draft, prepared on 3 November 2026, uses the September 2026 Kenya EBD (`ebd_KE_smp_relSep-2026.zip`), AviList v2026, and the official `eBird_Taxonomy_v2026-october-2026.csv`. There is one eBird taxonomy input. The original official CSV is pinned by path and SHA-256; it is not reconstructed from the names workbook or API. Published v2026.0 assets remain unchanged.

The checklist contains **1,149 species: 1,144 native and five naturalized**. Buffy Pipit (*Anthus vaalensis*, `bufpip1`) is the sole addition to national-list membership, supported by five clustered observations dated 10 October 2015 to 24 December 2022. This is newly available source evidence, not evidence of a new arrival. No national-list species are removed.

All 4,948,157 source observation rows are accounted for: 4,919,970 reportable-species records, 4,901 supplementary-entity records, and 23,286 excluded unidentified records. Species evidence produces 895,561 spatially clustered observations, compared with 875,099 in the published v2026.0 snapshot. Differences between source snapshots and taxonomic concepts are not population trends.

Four explicit, September-scoped source corrections reconcile 42,877 records with the official taxonomy. The 8,174 broad Spotted/Mediterranean Flycatcher rows remain unidentified; only the 44 identified striata-group rows support Spotted Flycatcher, yielding 39 clustered observations. Old Little Swift records join the broader 2026 Little/House Swift lump. Old Rufous-naped Lark records receive the documented Kenyan assignment; this is a regional interpretation rather than global concept equivalence. Original source IDs are retained, and target IDs/categories, reasons and record counts appear in `audit/ebird_taxon_corrections.csv`. Every unlisted ID/category mismatch still stops the build. See [the taxonomy audit](../taxonomy-2026-audit.md).

The source-taxonomy corrections are separate from eBird-to-AviList exceptions and exotic-status corrections. African Swamphen and Siberian Stonechat retain their maintained eBird-to-AviList exceptions. Thirty Rock Dove and three Rose-ringed Parakeet records receive the existing, explicitly September-scoped exotic-code corrections. Raw source files remain unchanged. The compaction cache includes checksums for the official taxonomy and both correction tables.

The comparison with v2026.0 has 995 material change groups: one national-list addition, 423 taxonomy/name/classification changes, 569 evidence changes, and two category changes. The Little Swift and Spotted Flycatcher concept transitions are represented as one-to-one checklist successors, with old and new IDs retained. These links describe a broader or narrower taxon; they do not imply concept equivalence or reassign observations. The standalone comparison JSON embeds their rationale. Reproduce it with:

```sh
uv run python scripts/compare_releases.py \
  release-assets/v2026.0/checklist.csv dist/v2026.1/checklist.csv \
  dist/v2026.1/comparison \
  --concept-successors data/curation/taxonomy_2026.0_to_2026.1.csv
```

Black-rumped Buttonquail now has four clustered observations and receives `RAR`. Collared Flycatcher has a record dated 25 September 2026 and no longer receives `HIST`. Derived status uses the reference date 30 September 2026. The evidence bounding coordinates are unchanged; the metadata record count is updated to 4,919,970.

The canonical PDF and website preserve 13 unresolved historical additions and add Buffy Pipit, yielding 14 species displayed as Pending EARC. This is an occurrence-review flag and does not determine checklist membership. The separate November 2026 editorial draft continues to hide that display, as configured in its profile.

Generated products include the checklist CSV and manifest, 22-page canonical checklist PDF, November draft PDF, release comparison CSV/JSON/Markdown/HTML, GBIF/IPT export, and website. The GBIF package contains 1,149 species in 1,716 linked Taxon core rows. Five public files are staged under `release-assets/v2026.1/`; the complete local bundle is under `dist/v2026.1/`. The historical comparison PDF remains available in the archived website comparison directory; the generic release comparison provides CSV and an interactive HTML report rather than a new comparison PDF.

Validation passed for official taxonomy parsing, source corrections and source-ID retention, unidentified evidence, cache invalidation, release-pipeline fixtures, release tables and current eBird codes, publication metadata, GBIF/IPT export, EARC decisions, historical checklists and mappings, endemic categories, asset staging, policy fixtures, and the assembled website. The canonical PDF cover, middle and final checklist pages and the editorial draft cover/introduction were rendered and visually inspected. The optional cross-project atlas/comparison coherence audit was not run because those sibling repositories are absent from this workspace.
