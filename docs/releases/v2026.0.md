# Checklist of the Birds of Kenya v2026.0

This release, published on 20 November 2026 with an editorial cutoff of 3 November 2026, uses the September 2026 Kenya EBD (`ebd_KE_smp_relSep-2026.zip`), AviList v2026, and the official `eBird_Taxonomy_v2026-october-2026.csv`. There is one eBird taxonomy input. The original official CSV is pinned by path and SHA-256; it is not reconstructed from the names workbook or API. It supersedes v2025.0, whose published assets remain unchanged in the archive.

The checklist contains **1,148 species: 1,143 native and five naturalized**. National checklist membership matches v2025.0. Buffy Pipit (*Anthus vaalensis*, `bufpip1`) is recognized by AviList and is held in [the checklist exclusions](../../data/curation/releases/v2026.0/checklist_exclusions.csv) pending acceptance of the Kenyan records by the East African Rarities Committee. Its six source records correspond to five clustered observations dated 10 October 2015 to 24 December 2022.

All 4,948,157 source observation rows are accounted for: 4,919,970 reportable-species records, 4,901 supplementary-entity records, and 23,286 excluded unidentified records. Species evidence produces 895,556 spatially clustered observations for included species, compared with 875,099 in the published v2025.0 snapshot. Differences between source snapshots and taxonomic concepts are not population trends.

Four explicit, September-scoped source corrections reconcile 42,877 records with the official taxonomy. The 8,174 broad Spotted/Mediterranean Flycatcher rows remain unidentified; only the 44 identified striata-group rows support Spotted Flycatcher, yielding 39 clustered observations. Old Little Swift records join the broader 2026 Little/House Swift lump. Old Rufous-naped Lark records receive the documented Kenyan assignment; this is a regional interpretation rather than global concept equivalence. Original source IDs are retained, and target IDs/categories, reasons and record counts appear in `audit/ebird_taxon_corrections.csv`. Every unlisted ID/category mismatch still stops the build. See [the taxonomy audit](../taxonomy-2026-audit.md).

The source-taxonomy corrections are separate from eBird-to-AviList exceptions and exotic-status corrections. African Swamphen and Siberian Stonechat retain their maintained eBird-to-AviList exceptions. Thirty Rock Dove and three Rose-ringed Parakeet records receive the existing, explicitly September-scoped exotic-code corrections. Raw source files remain unchanged. The compaction cache includes checksums for the official taxonomy and both correction tables.

The website now compares frozen taxonomic snapshots for any two registered versions, in either direction. Only names, classification and reviewed Avibase concept links enter comparisons. Species additions and removals are included. Observation counts, dates, occurrence categories and manual review decisions are excluded. Concept links come from the shared [taxon concept relationships](../taxon-concept-relationships.md); the release freezes all of its curated inputs. See [the release architecture](../release-architecture.md).

Black-rumped Buttonquail now has four clustered observations and receives `RAR`. Collared Flycatcher has a record dated 25 September 2026 and no longer receives `HIST`. Derived status uses the reference date 30 September 2026. The evidence bounding coordinates are unchanged; the metadata record count is updated to 4,919,970.

The canonical PDF and website show 13 unresolved historical additions as Pending EARC. This is an occurrence-review flag and does not determine checklist membership. The separate November 2026 editorial draft continues to hide that display, as configured in its profile.

Generated products include the checklist CSV and manifest, 22-page canonical checklist PDF, November draft PDF, taxonomy comparison JSON/CSV for every selected pair, GBIF/IPT export, and website. The sealed bundle, including one canonical PDF, taxonomy snapshot and version-specific curation, is staged under `release-assets/v2026.0/`; the complete local bundle is under `dist/v2026.0/`. Comparison PDF support has been removed. Older checklist PDFs remain available through the website archive; the 2019 original printed edition is clearly labeled.

Validation passed for official taxonomy parsing, source corrections and source-ID retention, unidentified evidence, cache invalidation, release-pipeline fixtures, release tables and current eBird codes, publication metadata, GBIF/IPT export, EARC decisions, historical checklists and mappings, endemic categories, asset staging, policy fixtures, and the assembled website. The canonical PDF cover, middle and final checklist pages and the editorial draft cover/introduction were rendered and visually inspected. The optional cross-project atlas/comparison coherence audit was not run because those sibling repositories are absent from this workspace.

v2026.0 is the active website release. Its GitHub Release carries the canonical PDF, checklist CSV and manifest. The existing GBIF/IPT resource is updated separately.
