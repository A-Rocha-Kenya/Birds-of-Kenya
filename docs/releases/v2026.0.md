# Checklist of the Birds of Kenya v2026.0

This versioned release contains 1,148 bird species represented in Kenya, following AviList v2026 and based on the June 2026 eBird Basic Dataset, curated checklist membership, sensitive species, and EARC evidence. It continues the historical checklist series without assigning a numbered edition.

The release includes 1,143 native and five naturalized species. Escapee-only observations, hybrids, domestic forms, and unidentified taxa are outside the main species checklist.

EARC decisions annotate national-record review and do not determine taxonomic validity. Pending review is retained for a later version. Ruddy Shelduck and Brown Skua are included with their rejected-record annotations; a rejection of a record no longer removes the species and its source evidence. Categories remain under editorial review in the repository and are omitted from the GBIF taxonomy export.

The comparison is **From the 2019.1 to 2026.0**, with 375 change groups. These groups distinguish taxonomy/name changes from additions and removals; an addition does not necessarily indicate a new arrival in Kenya.

The GBIF input contains 1,148 species in 1,715 Taxon core rows, including the linked higher-taxon hierarchy. The separate October 2026 draft PDF is an editorial variant; the canonical PDF is labeled v2026.0.

Validation: release pipeline and tables, EARC record-review annotations and retention, endemic categories, publication metadata, GBIF table, legacy migration, asset staging, historical checklists, and website. Source files are pinned and hashed, including the EARC decisions. The build reuses the matching compacted EBD cache.

The GBIF checklist is published under DOI [10.15468/mehwcx](https://doi.org/10.15468/mehwcx). Later versions update the existing IPT resource and registered dataset; see [the update guide](../gbif-publication.md).

Development revision 1 updates AviList to v2026 and current eBird codes to the 27 October 2026 reference. The EBD month remains June 2026, with its matching source taxonomy retained. The identified striata subset now supplies Spotted Flycatcher evidence; undifferentiated pre-split records remain in review audits. The corrected lark crosswalk associates Athi with old Rufous-naped Lark, not old Red-winged Lark. Rebuilt local assets are prepared for review; this does not publish a new GBIF version.

The refreshed local bundle is a development draft dated 31 October 2026. The existing dataset DOI is retained, and publication remains a separate explicit step.
